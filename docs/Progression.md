# Learning Progression for Slovingo-de-fr

## Philosophy

Kids learn best when they:
1. **See progress quickly** — start with useful phrases they can actually say
2. **Have fun** — learning feels like play, not homework
3. **Build confidence** — success breeds motivation
4. **Repeat naturally** — vocabulary gets reused, not hammered

This document outlines our pedagogical approach.

---

## Overall structure

**Phases:**
- **Phase 1: Survival Kit** (Series 00) — 5 fiches of essential phrases
- **Phase 2: Daily Life** (Series 01-03) — Familie, Haus, Essen (~15 fiches)
- **Phase 3: Beyond Home** (Series 04-06) — Stadt, Tiere, Spiele (~15 fiches)
- **Phase 4: Extension** (Series 07+) — Holidays, hobbies, deeper culture

Each series = 1-2 weeks of casual learning (5-6 fiches, one per day + rest days).

---

## Vocabulary strategy

### Core vocab per series: ~14 words

- **7 essential**: Words used in every exercise, heavily reinforced
- **7 complementary**: Related words, less frequently drilled

### Cumulative learning

Each series must:
- Reuse all vocabulary from previous series in its fiche 05 (dialogue)
- Reuse most vocabulary from the current series in fiche 06 (extra)
- Keep phrases short (max 5-6 words) for retention

### Example: Series 01 — Familie (Family)

**Essential vocab:**
- Mutter (mother), Vater (father), Bruder (brother), Schwester (sister)
- Oma (grandmother), Opa (grandfather)
- Ich heiße... (My name is...), Wie heißt du? (What's your name?)

**Complementary vocab:**
- Eltern (parents), Familie (family)
- Großeltern (grandparents), Tante (aunt), Onkel (uncle)
- Schwester / Brüderchen (affectionate diminutives)

**Grammar:**
- Subject pronouns: ich, du, er, sie
- Simple present: "Ich bin...", "Du heißt...", "Er/Sie heißt..."
- Possessives: mein, meine, dein, deine (introduced, not exhaustive)

## Difficulty progression

### Series 00: Kit de Survie — AABB (Beginner)

**Focus:** Hello, goodbye, thanks, politeness, one simple question

- Very short phrases (1-3 words)
- Pronunciation emphasis
- No grammar explanations (just patterns)
- Lots of encouragement

**Example:**
```
! Hallo!
> Bonjour !
```

### Series 01-02: Foundations — A1+ (Early Elementary)

**Focus:** Introduce verbs, pronouns, and practical daily vocab

- Phrases up to 5-6 words
- Simple present tense (sein, heißen, mögen)
- Survival vocab + family, house, food
- More complex audio cards but repetitive

**Example:**
```
! Ich heiße Anna und ich bin 8 Jahre alt.
> Je m'appelle Anna et j'ai 8 ans.
> Ich heiße Anna = Je m'appelle Anna (I am called Anna)
> und = et (and)
> ich bin = j'ai (I am)
> 8 Jahre alt = 8 ans (8 years old)
```

### Series 03-04: Consolidation — A1+/A2

**Focus:** Past tense, questions, negations, more complex dialogues

- Up to 7-8 words per phrase
- Preterite or simple past introduced gently
- Series vocab + city, animals, sports
- Dialogues become more natural, less repetitive

### Series 05+: Extension — A2

**Focus:** Deeper culture, longer texts, personal expression

- Kids can form own simple sentences
- Culture corner becomes richer (traditions, famous people, games)
- Optional: video dialogues, pen-pal sections, creative writing

## Daily routine (1 week per series)

### Day 1: Fiche 01
- New vocab + grammar point 1
- 6-8 short audio cards
- **Task:** Repeat, listen, copy pronunciation
- **Time:** 10-15 mins

### Day 2: Fiche 02
- New vocab + grammar point 2
- Review words from Fiche 01
- 6-8 audio cards (mix old + new)
- **Task:** Quick quiz (visual matching, listen-and-repeat)
- **Time:** 10-15 mins

### Day 3: Rest / Exploration
- Free play with audio cards
- Exercises on Slovingo (if available)
- Listen to Fiche 03 preview (optional)

### Day 4: Fiche 03
- Combine points from 01 + 02
- New vocabulary (less essential, more fun)
- 6-8 audio cards
- **Task:** Speak along with cards

### Day 5: Fiche 04
- Mix everything so far
- Cultural corner (fun fact about Germany/Austria/Switzerland)
- 6-8 audio cards
- **Task:** Can you say these phrases?

### Day 6: Fiche 05 — Dialogue
- Meet the characters!
- 10-12 audio cards in one continuous story
- All vocabulary is known (or marked "new")
- **Task:** Read with audio, act out with friend/parent

### Day 7: Fiche 06 — Extra
- Vocabulary recap table
- 15-20 audio cards with no new words
- Exercise foundation
- **Task:** Do exercises, play games, build streak

### Day 8+: Repeat / Explore
- Series review games
- Ready for next series

## Character-driven narrative

To make this less textbook-y, introduce recurring characters in Series 00:

- **Lea** (8 years old, German) — protagonist
- **Tom** (9 years old, German) — friend
- **Oma Hilde** — Lea's grandmother
- **Maybe a pet** 🐶

They appear in fiche 05 (dialogue) starting from Series 01, and later in cultural sections ("Oma Hilde teaches you...").

## Motivation & progress tracking

Even without a full gamification system, structure should support:

1. **Daily check-ins** ("Did I learn today?" Yes → feel good)
2. **Weekly completion** (Series done → badge/star)
3. **Vocabulary mastery** (Know all X words → feel smart)
4. **Streak tracking** (Days in a row → build habit)

*Note:* Code updates can add these over time without changing content format.

---

## Assessment & iteration

### After each series:
- Does the child understand the vocab?
- Can they form simple sentences with it?
- Are they engaged? (tone, pace, illustrations)
- What illustrations work best?

### Pivot points:
- If 05 dialogue feels too hard → simplify Fiches 01-04
- If 06 extra is boring → vary exercise types more
- If grammar intro is confusing → keep it even shorter
- If illustrations don't land → refresh

The format is intentionally flexible so we can evolve based on real feedback.
